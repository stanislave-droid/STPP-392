About the Game

A puzzle journey to rebuild a fallen kingdom

Piggy Kingdom follows a friendly pig on a mission to restore his lost castle through hundreds of colorful match-3 levels.

Players connect and clear gems and objects to complete level goals, earning rewards used to rebuild and decorate the kingdom.
